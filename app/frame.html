Spacing scale

4px is the only fine step. Everything above it moves in 8s: 0, 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 72. Type line-heights below are pulled from this same scale — one rhythm for layout and type.

Color

Dark, deliberately this time (an earlier dark-page pass was a misread and got reverted — this one was asked for specifically, with its own direction). Page and cards both live in the charcoal register now; cards read as "raised" one step lighter. Filled/active chips (buttons, active toggle states) flip to a light paper fill to still pop off an all-dark page. Pink is unified now, not overlap-exclusive — one `--color-accent` value colors the overlap marker, the wordmark's "Gig," and the header avatar's hover state; "Herd" keeps its own dedicated green. No glow anywhere, logo included — a glow pass on "Herd" was tried and cut. Army green is atmosphere only — it dropped out of the avatar-color palette when that set got retuned to the logo's own tone/value.

Grid: 4 / 8 / 12

4 columns mobile, 8 tablet, 12 desktop — the column count itself changes, not just margin and gutter. That means "half" and "quarter" are semantic classes, not fixed span numbers: .col-half is 2 of 4 on mobile, 4 of 8 on tablet, 6 of 12 on desktop — always exactly half. Resize the window to watch the columns, margin, and gutter all shift together at 600px and 1024px. Toggle the grid button (bottom right) to overlay it on this page.

Measure check

Body text at 16px targets a 60–75 character line. On desktop, full-width (12 columns) runs too long to read comfortably — .col-half lands closer to the right measure. Use .prose alongside it for any real paragraph so the cap holds even if the column gets wider.

This paragraph sits in .col-half and is capped with .prose so it stays around a 65-character measure regardless of how wide its column gets. It should read comfortably at every breakpoint without needing a media query to fix it.
